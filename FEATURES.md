# FuseHealth Dashboard — What You Get

A plain-language tour of the dashboard, **page by page**, so you can check each
screen and tell us what's good and what to change. No technical knowledge needed.

> **How to read the status tags**
> 🟢 **Live** — working now with your real Google data
> 🟡 **Needs a quick switch on your side** — one setting/permission and it turns on
> 🔌 **Needs an account connected** — switches on once you give us access to that tool

---

## How the dashboard works (the basics)

- **Everything is saved inside the dashboard**, so every page opens **instantly** — no waiting.
- **Pick the website** you want to look at from the **dropdown at the top** — the whole dashboard
  follows your choice.
- **Pick the time range** at the top: **last 1 day / 7 days / 30 days**. Every page updates to match.
- **Refresh** pulls the newest data from Google and your tools and saves it. There's a **Refresh All**
  button, and each page has its own refresh. A **live progress bar** shows each source loading.
- Until you press Refresh, you keep seeing the **last saved data**.

## The pages

### 1. Overview — your snapshot 🟢
The one screen that answers "how are we doing and what needs attention?"
- **Four headline numbers** — clicks, impressions, click-rate (CTR), average Google ranking — each
  with an **up/down vs. the previous period**.
- **Decision Signals** — plain-English callouts generated from your own data, e.g.
  *"CTR declined 45% — review your title tags and meta descriptions."*
- **30-day traffic graph** — clicks and impressions over time.
- **AI Weekly Summary** — now laid out in clear sections: **Critical Issues to Fix**,
  **Wins to Maintain**, and an **Overall Assessment** (no more wall of text).
- **Top pages**, an **ads summary**, **top keywords**, and a **competitor positioning** snapshot.

### 2. SEO Performance — where the easy wins are 🟢
Built to point you at actions, not just numbers.
- **Attention summary** at the top — counts of low-CTR opportunities, traffic anomalies, and
  critical issues.
- **"Pages seen but not clicked"** — pages with lots of impressions but very few clicks (under 2%).
  These are your **fastest title/description rewrite wins**.
- **Recent traffic anomalies** — days where clicks or impressions were unusually high or low.
- **Technical issues** — 404 (broken) pages, overly long URLs, and redirects, each with a short
  explanation of why it matters.
- **Performance by country and device** for context.

### 3. Keywords — find and prioritise SEO opportunities 🟢
- **Headline numbers** — keywords tracked, average position, total search volume, total clicks.
- **Keyword Health Score** — one number for how healthy your keyword set is.
- **Action buckets** you can act on today:
  - **Quick Wins** — already ranking 4–10, a small push reaches the top.
  - **Striking Distance** — page-2 keywords close to breaking through.
  - **Declining** — keywords slipping that need attention.
  - **High impressions, low CTR** — seen a lot, clicked little.
- **Search intent** and **keyword difficulty** breakdowns, plus the **full keyword table**
  (volume, difficulty, cost-per-click, position, intent).

### 4. Page Health — how each page is doing 🟢 *(speed needs 🟡)*
- **Site Health Score** plus **every page** with clicks, impressions, CTR, position, and whether
  **Google has indexed** it.
- **Critical-issue lists** — pages **not indexed**, pages **getting views but no clicks**, and (once
  speed is switched on) **slow pages**.
- 🟡 **Page speed** (mobile + desktop) turns on as soon as the PageSpeed setting is enabled on your
  Google key — the column then fills in automatically.

### 5. Positioning — rankings and competitors 🟢
A rank-tracker view.
- **Rankings distribution** — how many keywords sit in the top 3, 4–10, 11–20, and beyond.
- **Improved / Declined / New / Lost** keyword counts vs. the previous period.
- **Visibility trend** over time and **top movers**.
- **Full rankings table** with position changes, and your **competitors** and where they overlap
  with you.

### 6. Backlinks — who links to your site 🔌
- Totals: backlinks, **live vs. lost**, referring domains, average domain quality.
- A full, searchable list of every link.
- 🔌 *Switches on once the backlink tool account is connected (currently shows an empty state).*

### 7. Ads — paid advertising results 🔌
- Spend, clicks, conversions, cost-per-click, CTR, and **ROAS** (return on ad spend).
- **Per-campaign** breakdown by platform.
- 🔌 *Switches on once Google Ads / Meta accounts are connected (currently shows an empty state).*

### 8. Alerts & Audit — your automatic watchdog 🟢
The all-in-one health check.
- **Metric anomalies** — unusual click/impression days, which you can **acknowledge** once reviewed.
- **Indexing status** — a one-line summary (*e.g. "44 indexed · 5 returning 404 · 1 not indexed"*),
  then a per-page list with problems shown first, in plain words (no technical codes).
- **PageSpeed issues** (once speed is switched on) and the **technical issues** list.

### 9. Settings — connections and websites 🟢
- **Active website** and the **switcher** to change which site the dashboard shows.
- **Connected APIs** — which data sources are working and when they last updated.
- **Refresh All Data** and your **preferences**.
